    2022

    Truffles

    Co-founder and CTO, Singapore

    Feb 2022 to Feb 2024

    Truffles was a B2B neobank for small international businesses: issue an invoice, have the buyer accept it, and get paid across borders in minutes instead of days. I was co-founder and CTO until February 2024. The company has since moved to credit-intelligence software. [23]

    1. Feb 2022

      Co-founded Truffles and became CTO. [01]

    2. 21 Mar 2023

      Selected for the first cohort of Outlier Ventures' Zero Knowledge Base Camp. [02]

    3. 4 Apr 2023

      Truffles announced its V1 payment platform: 10 geographies, 13 currencies, settlement in about 15 minutes. Invoice acceptance and payment were proved with invoice NFTs, and KYC and AML screening used privacy-preserving proofs. [03]

    4. 23 May 2023

      Truffles announced its NFT invoicing, deployed on Polygon's infrastructure. [24]

    5. 1 Jun 2023

      Co-authored a paper on using Polygon Supernets for cross-border trade payments, with Truffles' Evan Winter and six others. [15]

      arXiv:2306.00439

      Examination of Supernets to Facilitate International Trade for Indian Exports to Brazil

      Examines the India to Brazil trade corridor, where compliance, speed and payments are the obstacles, and proposes a digital solution on Polygon Supernets with trade and banking stakeholders as permissioned nodes. Preprint, 22 pages, econ.GN.

      Evan Winter, Anupam Shah, Ujjwal Gupta, Anshul Kumar, Deepayan Mohanty, Juan Carlos Uribe, Aishwary Gupta, Mini P. Thomas.

      Read on arXivPDF
    6. 19 Jun 2023

      Outlier Ventures reported the angel round closed during the program.

    2024

    Chainrisk

    Co-founder and CTO; co-founder of RiskLayer

    Feb 2024 to May 2025

    An economic-security and risk-management firm for DeFi. It stress-tests protocols with simulation, then recommends the risk parameters that keep them solvent without wasting capital. RiskLayer was its EigenLayer product.

    1. 6 Dec 2023

      Chainrisk closed a pre-seed round from Antler, before my time at the company.

    2. Feb 2024

      Joined as co-founder and CTO.

    3. 2024

      Chainrisk published an economic audit of Compound V3's USDC market on Arbitrum One, using simulation to recommend collateral parameters. [07]

    4. Jul 2024

      RiskLayer closed its Builders Round, co-led by Antler and Momentum6. The company's release lists Compound, Angle Labs, Gyroscope, Ebisu Finance, Arbitrum and Fuel Network among the protocols it served. [06]

    5. 2025

      RiskLayer has since become AFI. [22]

    6. May 2025

      Left Chainrisk. [01]

    Now

    Same subject throughout: money infrastructure, where errors are costly.

    A risk engine and hedges for AI's GPU debt. It prices the covenant floor on GPU-backed loans and stress-tests the loan under simulated collateral paths. The live pricer runs in the browser.
