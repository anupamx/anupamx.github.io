      Examination of Supernets to Facilitate International Trade for Indian Exports to Brazil

      Examines the India to Brazil trade corridor, where compliance, speed and payments are the obstacles, and proposes a digital solution on Polygon Supernets with trade and banking stakeholders as permissioned nodes. Preprint, 22 pages.

      Evan Winter, Anupam Shah, Ujjwal Gupta, Anshul Kumar, Deepayan Mohanty, Juan Carlos Uribe, Aishwary Gupta, Mini P. Thomas. The first four list Truffles Tech Pte Ltd as their affiliation. [23]

    2024

    Chainrisk

    Co-founder and CTO, then Strategic Advisor; co-founder of RiskLayer

    Feb 2024 to May 2025

    An economic-security and risk-management firm for DeFi. It stress-tests protocols with simulation, then recommends the risk parameters that keep them solvent without wasting capital. RiskLayer was its security product built on EigenLayer, a restaking protocol.

    1. Feb 2024

      Joined Chainrisk as co-founder and CTO.

    2. 2024 to 2025

      Worked on capital formation and business development at Chainrisk. I took part in the RiskLayer Builders Round and built relationships with LPs.

    3. 2024

      Chainrisk published an economic audit of Compound V3's USDC market on Arbitrum One, using simulation to recommend collateral parameters. [06] [29]

    4. Jul 2024

      RiskLayer closed its Builders Round, co-led by Antler and Momentum6. The company's release lists Compound, Angle Labs, Gyroscope, Ebisu Finance, Arbitrum and Fuel Network among the protocols and ecosystems it served. [05]

    5. 2025

      RiskLayer's profiles now point to Artificial Financial Intelligence (AFI). [17]

    Now

    Money infrastructure, where errors are costly.

    Ballast

    Ballast is an independent risk engine for GPU-backed debt. A lender enters a loan and gets its breach probability and timing, the peak loan-to-value over the life of the debt, expected loss under assumptions the lender sets, and the advance rate the chips support. The live pricer runs in the browser.
