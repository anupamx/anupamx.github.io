Chainrisk

Co-founder and CTO; co-founder of RiskLayer

Feb 2024 to May 2025

An economic-security and risk-management firm for DeFi. It stress-tests protocols with simulation, then recommends the risk parameters that keep them solvent without wasting capital.

  • Reports $5B+ in assets secured across 10+ protocols, with a Rust-based simulation engine behind the work. [05]
  • Chainrisk published the economic audit of Compound V3 on Arbitrum One, stress-testing the USDC market to set collateral thresholds that hold through market crashes. [07]
  • RiskLayer, the EigenLayer AVS built by Chainrisk Labs, closed a Builders Round co-led by Antler and Momentum6 in July 2024. The same release reports $10B+ in assets managed to date, across Compound, Angle Labs, Gyroscope, Ebisu Finance, Arbitrum and Fuel Network. [06]
  • The Economic Times credits my capital formation and business development work at Chainrisk Labs. [14]

Truffles

Co-founder and CTO, Singapore

Feb 2022 to Feb 2024 [01]

A B2B neobank for small international businesses: issue an invoice, have the buyer accept it, and get paid across borders in minutes instead of days. I led the technology.

  • $60M+ of payment volume processed. [04]
  • Selected for the first cohort of Outlier Ventures' Zero Knowledge Base Camp, March 2023. [02]
  • V1 payment platform launched in 2023, covering 10 geographies and 13 currencies, with settlement in roughly 15 minutes. Invoice acceptance and payment were proved cryptographically with invoice NFTs, and KYC and AML screening used privacy-preserving proofs. [03]
  • Co-authored the Polygon Supernets paper on cross-border trade payments, below. [15]

Research

Cross-border payments on Polygon Supernets.

A paper on a more efficient cross-border payment and document-handling process, co-authored with Truffles' Evan Winter and six others. Preprint on arXiv.

arXiv:2306.00439

Examination of Supernets to Facilitate International Trade for Indian Exports to Brazil

General Economics (econ.GN)
22 pages, June 2023
doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2306.00439

The paper examines the India to Brazil trade corridor, where compliance, speed and payments are the obstacles. It then proposes a digital solution built on Polygon Supernets, with trade and banking stakeholders onboarded as permissioned nodes, aimed at Indian exporters who need a lawful channel for hard-currency payments. [15]

Authors: Evan Winter, Anupam Shah, Ujjwal Gupta, Anshul Kumar, Deepayan Mohanty, Juan Carlos Uribe, Aishwary Gupta, Mini P. Thomas.
